# Getting started with Better Workspace

[Download advanced Mac 1.0](https://github.com/ZSturman/Better-Workspace-Public/releases/tag/v1.0) · [Explore the browser demo](https://zacharysturman.com/better-workspace/demo) · [Portfolio](https://zacharysturman.com/projects/better-workspace)

Better Workspace keeps projects, tasks and ideas close to the next thing you want to do. It is for people juggling creative, personal or technical projects.

## Choose your edition

| Edition | Best for | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Mac — direct download | Existing folders, portable plans, project priorities, capture, work history and optional integrations | macOS 14.4+, Apple silicon or Intel |
| Native App Store app — release status on the portfolio | Personal projects, tasks, ideas and documents, offline work and private iCloud sync across devices | macOS 14.4+, iOS/iPadOS 17+ |

The native Store app works independently. It does not need the advanced Mac app or its background service. The optional connected mobile workspace uses the advanced Mac and the same Apple account. Apple review and public listing availability are separate from the direct Mac release.

## Install advanced Mac

1. Download **BetterWorkspace-1.0-8-mac.zip** from the release's Assets section.
2. Double-click the ZIP in Downloads. Drag **Better Workspace.app** into Applications.
3. Open Better Workspace from Applications. Accept macOS's normal first-launch confirmation.
4. In **Settings → General**, choose your workspace and inbox folders. Start with a new empty folder if you want to explore safely.
5. In **Settings → Services**, install/start the bundled background service to enable its planning metadata and work-history features. Python and dependencies are included; no Terminal setup is needed.

Grant folder access when macOS asks for the folders you chose. Notifications and login startup are optional. Notion and agent tools are optional and have their own setup. Do not disable Gatekeeper to install this release; the app is signed, notarized and stapled.

## Find your first next step

1. Add a project folder under your workspace's Projects area, or browse existing projects. Review any project setup prompt.
2. Open a project and add a task or portable work plan. Give it a priority and choose a small next action.
3. Keep early possibilities in Ideas; promote an idea after reviewing where it should go.
4. Use Quick Capture / Ingest for a thought, link or file. Review it and choose its destination.
5. Return to Overview to choose what to do next. Project work history and progress help you pick up where you left off.

## Personal workspace on Mac, iPhone and iPad

When the Store version is available, install it through Apple's listing. Choose **Personal Workspace**, then **Create my first project**. Add a task, idea or document and link it to your project. Complete tasks, search your records, or archive finished work. **Try a private sample** offers editable examples that disappear when you close them.

Personal records save locally and synchronize through your private iCloud account when available. Use the same Apple account on your devices. Transfers may take a few minutes; check the status in My Workspace. Offline edits stay local until reconnection.

For optional connected folder browsing/capture, enable mobile publishing on the advanced Mac, enable iCloud Drive on mobile, and leave the Mac online to apply queued planning changes. The native Store workspace and the connected folder workspace are separate experiences.
